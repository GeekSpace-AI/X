# 第二册编写计划（Day 31–60）

第二册的编写蓝图：每天的场景、中英差异点、指定新词、指定短语和锚点句。`build.py` 只读取 `day*.txt`，本文件不进入 PDF。

## 通用规则

- **新词不重复**：第一册已注释的 573 个词和短语（见第一册附录 1，`python3 build.py check` 会自动比对）在第二册**不再注释**，只在新语境中自然复现。下表中每个词 / 短语只在指定的那一天注释。
- **锚点句**：每天 5 个锚点句必须**原样**出现在当天的【C】故事里。第二天的热身从前一天的锚点句取 3–4 句，再从 3–7 天前取 1–2 句；**每天热身里至少 1 句来自第一册**（重点复现第一册的薄弱项：第三人称 -s、过去式、否定问句回答、a / the、because / so 连接）。
- **故事长度**（英文词数）：D31–36 约 180–200；D38–43 约 190–215；D45–50 约 200–230；D52–59 约 220–260；复习日约 200–260。
- **听力**：第二册用正常语速，四步听法改为「关键词听 → 不看文字听 → 看英文听 → 段落听写」（见 `book.yaml` 的 `listen_steps`）。听写从单句改为 2–3 句的段落，`@dict` 写成 `S5–S7` 这样的范围。
- **人物**：林晓 Lily（入职第二个月）、张涛 Tom、Emma、吴佳 Grace、李凯 Kevin、Mike（视频）、实习生 Leo；新角色：小梅 Mei（林晓的大学同学，Day 7 出现过）、林晓的妈妈 Mom（视频通话）、房东 Landlord、中介 Agent、医生 Doctor、银行柜员 Teller、店员 Clerk、地铁工作人员 Staff。外部角色在故事里用单个英文词作说话人。
- **补充资源**（`@res`，可选）：只写「在 YouTube 频道 Rachel's English 内搜索 "……"」或「在 youglish.com 输入 "……"，选 US」，**不编造任何具体视频链接**。

## 每天的安排

### Day 31 · 你在深圳住多久了 / How Long Have You Lived Here?（第 1 周）
- 场景：入职第二个月的第一个周一，午饭时 Emma、Kevin 和林晓聊各自在深圳住了多久、在公司做了多久、彼此认识多久。
- 中英差异：①「我在深圳住了两年了」I've lived in Shenzhen for two years.（不说 I live in Shenzhen two years）② for + 一段时间 vs since + 起点 ③ How long have you been here?（已经多久）vs How long are you here?（还要待多久）④「我出生在湖南」I was born in Hunan.
- 新词（20）：since, live, hometown, born, college, graduate, local, dialect, accent, province, north, south, culture, language, foreign, climate, humid, summer, winter, decade
- 短语（5）：How long have you…?, grow up, move to, get to know, all my life
- 锚点句：How long have you lived in Shenzhen? ｜ I've lived here for two years. ｜ I've worked at SwiftLink for five weeks. ｜ I was born in Hunan, but I grew up in Changsha. ｜ Kevin and Tom have known each other since college.

### Day 32 · 你吃过……吗 / Have You Ever Tried It?（第 1 周）
- 场景：周五团队聚餐，林晓带大家去一家湖南菜馆；Emma 第一次尝臭豆腐，大家互相问「吃过……吗」。
- 中英差异：①「我吃过臭豆腐」I've tried stinky tofu.（「过」= have done）② Have you ever…? 回答 Yes, I have. / No, never. ③「我从来没……过」I've never…（不说 I never ate…）④ ever 用在疑问句，肯定句不说 ✗ I've ever been…
- 新词（20）：taste, flavor, sour, sweet, salty, bitter, smell, tofu, dish, pork, fish, vegetable, vegetarian, chopsticks, brave, experience, before, weird, recipe, allergic
- 短语（5）：Have you ever…?, for the first time, give it a try, not a big fan of, can't stand
- 锚点句：Have you ever tried stinky tofu? — No, I haven't. ｜ I've never eaten it before. ｜ It smells weird, but it tastes great. ｜ It's my first time. ｜ I'm not a big fan of sweet food.

### Day 33 · 你听说了吗 / Have You Heard?（第 1 周）
- 场景：周一早上，办公室里传开两条消息：吴佳订婚了；公司刚和一个大客户签了合同。
- 中英差异：①「他刚……」He's just… / He just…（just 的位置）②「我已经……了」I've already… ③ 问「……了吗」Have you … yet? / 答「还没」I haven't … yet ④「我刚知道」I just found out（不说 I just knew）
- 新词（20）：just, engaged, wedding, ring, propose, boyfriend, girlfriend, husband, wife, couple, announce, rumor, secret, puppy, adopt, cute, contract, officially, card, nobody
- 短语（5）：Have you heard…?, find out, get married, between you and me, Good for you
- 锚点句：Have you heard? Grace just got engaged! ｜ I've already sent her a message. ｜ Has Tom announced it yet? — Not yet. ｜ I just found out this morning. ｜ We've just signed a contract with a big new client.

### Day 34 · 我等了二十分钟了 / I've Been Waiting for Ages（第 1 周）
- 场景：周六，林晓在商场门口等小梅，小梅迟到了；两人逛街时聊小梅学车、林晓坚持学英语。
- 中英差异：①「我等了二十分钟了（还在等）」I've been waiting for twenty minutes. vs I waited for twenty minutes.（已经等完）②「我学英语学了十年了」I've been learning English for ten years. ③「好久」for ages / for a long time ④「我手机没电了」My phone died. / My battery is dead.（不说 My phone has no electricity）
- 新词（20）：mall, ages, license, lesson, instructor, fail, patient, impatient, battery, dead, entrance, exit, outside, café, dessert, bubble tea, finally, test, honestly, text
- 短语（5）：How long have you been…?, meet up, be running late, run out of, in a hurry
- 锚点句：I've been waiting for twenty minutes. ｜ Sorry, I'm running late. ｜ How long have you been learning to drive? ｜ My phone died, so I couldn't call you. ｜ I've been practicing English every day since I joined SwiftLink.

### Day 35 · 最近在忙什么 / What Have You Been Up To?（第 1 周）
- 场景：周日晚上，林晓和湖南老家的妈妈视频；妈妈问她最近忙什么，说起爸爸刚退休、奶奶每天跳广场舞。
- 中英差异：①「最近在忙什么？」What have you been up to (lately)? ②「最近」lately / recently + 现在完成时；these days + 一般现在时 ③「照顾」take care of；「保重」Take care. ④「我想你了」I miss you.（现在时；不说 I missed you）
- 新词（20）：mom, dad, grandma, grandpa, retire, healthy, health, dance, square, garden, cousin, aunt, uncle, relative, festival, lately, lonely, housework, grow, nephew
- 短语（5）：What have you been up to?, Long time no see, take care of, Take care, catch up
- 锚点句：What have you been up to lately? ｜ I've been really busy at work. ｜ Your dad has just retired. ｜ Grandma has been dancing in the square every evening. ｜ Take care, Mom. I miss you.

### Day 36 · 变化真大 / Things Have Changed（第 1 周）
- 场景：张涛和林晓去拜访客户的路上，张涛说起深圳这些年的变化；林晓说自己来深圳后胖了三公斤。
- 中英差异：①「深圳变化很大」Shenzhen has changed a lot.（不说 changes very big）②「房价涨了」Prices have gone up.（结果持续到现在）vs went up last year ③ ten years ago（某个时间点）vs for ten years / since 2015（一段时间）④「我胖了」I've put on weight.（「了」= 状态变化）
- 新词（20）：building, tower, population, increase, decrease, gain, lose, snack, modern, countryside, factory, farm, highway, bridge, village, fishing, huge, tiny, completely, area
- 短语（5）：go up, go down, compared with, over the years, put on weight
- 锚点句：Shenzhen has changed a lot. ｜ Forty years ago, this was a fishing village. ｜ Prices have gone up. ｜ I've put on three kilos since I moved here. ｜ Compared with my hometown, everything here is so fast.

### Day 37 · 第 1 周复习（复习日）
- 新情境：周六下午，林晓第一次去一个英语角，用英语介绍自己在深圳的经历、吃过的东西、最近的变化。
- 易混检查：for / since / ago；I've been to / I went；just / already / yet；I've been waiting（还在）vs I waited（结束）；lately vs these days。
- 新词（7）：topic, introduce, conversation, partner, volunteer, nickname, fluent ｜ 短语（1）：make friends

### Day 38 · 我正在做饭的时候 / I Was Cooking When the Power Went Out（第 2 周）
- 场景：周二晚上，林晓在合租屋里做饭时突然停电，邻居来敲门，第二天维修工来修好。
- 中英差异：①「我正在做饭的时候，停电了」I was cooking when the power went out.（长动作 was -ing + 短动作过去式）②「停电了」The power went out.（不说 stop electricity）③「我当时在洗澡」I was taking a shower.（当时正在）vs I took a shower.（做了这件事）④ 中文「正在」常省略，英文 was / were -ing 不能省
- 新词（20）：power, electricity, candle, flashlight, dark, scared, neighbor, knock, stairs, fridge, pot, stove, burn, smoke, alarm, suddenly, repairman, bath, towel, quickly
- 短语（5）：go out, turn on, in the dark, come back on, What were you doing?
- 锚点句：I was cooking dinner when the power went out. ｜ What were you doing when it happened? ｜ I was taking a shower. ｜ My neighbor knocked on the door. ｜ The power came back on at ten.

### Day 39 · 包落在地铁上了 / I Left My Backpack on the Subway（第 2 周）
- 场景：周三早上，林晓边打电话边把背包放在座位上，下车时忘了拿；她到地铁站的失物招领处描述背包。
- 中英差异：① while + 长动作（While I was talking…）vs when + 短动作 ② during + 名词（during the trip）vs while + 句子 ③「我把包落在地铁上了」I left my backpack on the subway.（left = 落下；不说 forgot my bag on…）④「它长什么样？」What does it look like?（不说 How does it look like?）
- 新词（20）：umbrella, wallet, backpack, key, staff, seat, describe, color, black, brown, gray, pocket, inside, brand, during, while, realize, honest, reward, careless
- 短语（5）：lost and found, hand in, What does it look like?, leave sth behind, as soon as
- 锚点句：I left my backpack on the subway. ｜ While I was talking on the phone, I put it on the seat. ｜ What does it look like? ｜ It's a black backpack with a small pocket in front. ｜ Someone handed it in this morning.

### Day 40 · 然后呢 / What Happened Next?（第 2 周）
- 场景：午饭时 Kevin 讲他入职第一天机房漏水的故事，大家不停追问「然后呢？」。
- 中英差异：①「后来」afterward / later / then 的区别 ②「然后呢？」What happened next? / And then? ③「最后（结果）……了」We ended up… ④「我设法……」I managed to…
- 新词（20）：server, pipe, leak, broken, wet, bucket, mop, panic, emergency, manage, save, luckily, shock, laugh, story, hero, boss, entire, nightmare, afterward
- 短语（5）：What happened next?, And then?, So what did you do?, first of all, end up
- 锚点句：What happened next? ｜ First of all, I turned off the power. ｜ So what did you do? ｜ We ended up working until midnight. ｜ Afterward, everyone called me a hero.

### Day 41 · 真的假的 / No Way!（第 2 周）
- 场景：周一，Emma 说她周末跑了第一个半程马拉松，还在机场遇到一位明星；吴佳也有好消息，林晓学着用各种反应语接话。
- 中英差异：①「真的假的？」No way! / Really? / Seriously?（不说 True or false?）②「太厉害了」That's amazing! / Impressive! / Way to go!（不说 You're so powerful）③ 表示感兴趣：重复关键词 + 追问（A marathon? How long did it take?）④「好羡慕」I'm so jealous!（玩笑语气）
- 新词（20）：marathon, run, actor, autograph, selfie, airport, crazy, unbelievable, awesome, exhausted, seriously, prize, win, medal, sore, leg, race, cheer, jealous, impressive
- 短语（5）：No way!, Are you kidding?, I can't believe…, Tell me more, Way to go!
- 锚点句：I ran my first half marathon! — No way! ｜ Are you kidding? That's amazing! ｜ How long did it take? ｜ I can't believe you met him at the airport. ｜ Tell me more!

### Day 42 · 怎么会这样 / How Come?（第 2 周）

- 中英差异：①「怎么会……？」How come + 陈述语序（How come the trip was canceled?）vs Why was…? ②「结果是……」It turned out (that)… ③「所以才……」That's why… ④「以防万一」just in case
- 新词（20）：typhoon, storm, wind, forecast, warning, beach, safe, dangerous, close, postpone, flight, signal, level, indoors, supermarket, shelf, empty, rush, explanation, flood
- 短语（5）：How come?, it turned out, That's why, stock up on, just in case

### Day 43 · 讲一个小故事 / Let Me Tell You a Story（第 2 周）
- 场景：周五团队聚餐，大家让林晓讲一个以前在快递网点工作时的故事：一位老人拿着一封写错地址的信来找她。
- 中英差异：① 讲故事的开头：One day, … / One time, …（「有一次」）②「突然」suddenly / all of a sudden ③「我很感动」I was touched. / I was moved.（不说 I was very moving）④ 讲过去的故事从头到尾用过去式（中文动词不变）
- 新词（20）：courier, parcel, pile, shift, overtime, elderly, granddaughter, cry, hug, grateful, search, memory, kindness, stranger, handwriting, smile, sticker, warm, touched, envelope
- 短语（5）：Let me tell you about…, one day, all of a sudden, it reminds me of, the best part was
- 锚点句：Let me tell you about my old job. ｜ One day, an old man came in with a letter. ｜ All of a sudden, he started to cry. ｜ I was really touched. ｜ It reminds me of my grandpa.

- 短语（5）：look for, living room, within walking distance, How much is the rent?, I'll take it
