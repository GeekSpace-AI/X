@diff: 「坐错车了」——I took the wrong bus.
@d_cn: 上错了公交、地铁，或者坐了反方向的车。
@d_en: I took the wrong bus. / I got off at the wrong stop. / It went in the opposite direction.
@d_why: 中文「坐错车」是「坐 + 错 + 车」，英文要换三个地方：①「坐」交通工具用 **take**（第一册 Day 2：take the subway），sit 只是「坐下」这个动作；②「错」不放在动词后面，而是用 **the wrong + 名词**：the wrong bus / the wrong stop / the wrong exit / the wrong way；③「车」要说具体：公交是 bus，地铁是 train，✗ the wrong car 听起来像「上错了别人的小汽车」。这个 the wrong + 名词 用处很多：「打错电话」I called the wrong number，「走错门」I went to the wrong door。
@d_when: 迟到时解释原因、告诉别人自己走错路了、提醒别人坐反了。
@d_vs: **I took the wrong bus.** 我坐错公交了。\n**I got off at the wrong stop.** 我下错站了。\n**We're going the wrong way.** 我们走反了。\n✗ I sat the wrong car. ✗ I took bus wrong.
@d_task: 用英文说：① 我下错站了。② 不好意思，我走错出口了。 | ① I got off at the wrong stop. ② Sorry, I took the wrong exit.
@diff: 「在……下车」——get off at…（不说 get down）
@d_cn: 上车、下车，以及在哪一站下。
@d_en: Get on the 26. Get off at the third stop. / Get in the taxi. / Let's get out here.
@d_why: 中文「上车」「下车」不管什么车都用「上 / 下」。英文按车的大小分：公交、地铁、火车、飞机这类**能在里面站着走**的，用 **get on / get off**；小汽车、出租车要弯腰**钻进去**，用 **get in / get out of**。✗ get down 是「蹲下、从高处下来」，美国人听到 get down the bus 会很困惑。「在哪站下」用 **at**：get off at the third stop / get off at Futian。
@d_when: 告诉别人坐哪路、在哪站下；车上问司机或乘客 Where should I get off?；打车到了说 Let's get out here.
@d_vs: **Get on the 26. Get off at the third stop.** 坐 26 路，第三站下。\n**Get in the taxi.** 上出租车吧。\n**Let's get out here.** 我们在这儿下吧。（出租车）\n✗ Get down at the third stop.
@d_task: 告诉 Leo：「在福田站下地铁，然后打个车。」 | Get off at Futian Station, then take a taxi.
@diff: 指路——Go straight. / Turn left at the traffic light. / It's on your right.
@d_cn: 告诉别人怎么走：直走、在哪儿转弯、经过什么、目的地在哪一边。
@d_en: Go straight and turn left at the traffic light. / Walk two blocks, past a hospital. / It's on your right.
@d_why: 中文指路常说「往前走，到红绿灯那儿往左一拐，就在你右手边」。英文指路用**祈使句**——动词开头，不加 you：Go… / Turn… / Walk…。方向说法很固定：**go straight**（✗ go straight forward）、**turn left**（✗ turn to left）、**on your right**（✗ in your right hand / ✗ at right side）。「在红绿灯处」用 **at**：at the traffic light / at the intersection；「经过」用 **past**：go past the hospital；美国人说距离常用 **blocks**，很少说「两百米」。
@d_when: 给问路的人、来访的客户、新同事指路；地图 App 的英文语音导航也用这些词。
@d_vs: **Go straight for two blocks.** 直走两个路口。\n**Turn left at the traffic light.** 在红绿灯左转。\n**Go past the hospital. It's on your right.** 走过医院，就在你右手边。\n✗ Go straight forward, turn to left, it's in your right hand.
@d_task: 给 Leo 指路：「直走，在第二个路口右转，咖啡馆在你左手边。」 | Go straight and turn right at the second intersection. The café is on your left.
@diff: 「在……对面」——across from…

@d_why: 中文「对面」直译常变成 ✗ It's in the opposite of the bank. 或 ✗ It's the bank's opposite.。美式口语最常用 **across from**：It's across from the bank.；想强调「隔着马路」就说 **across the street from**。opposite 作介词（opposite the bank）也对，但更偏英式、偏书面；美国人日常更多把 opposite 用作「相反的」：the opposite direction。across from 后面直接接地点：✗ across from the bank side。
