@diff: 「了」「过」「刚」「已经」「还没」——have done vs did；just / already / yet（复习 D32、D33、D36、D57）
@d_cn: 「我转正了」「我吃过」「我刚搬家」「我已经告诉她了」「还没」——中文动词不变，说不说具体时间都是同一个动词。
@d_en: I've just passed my probation! / I passed it on Thursday. / I've never seen such a beautiful sunrise. / Have you told Mom yet? — Not yet. / I've already told her.
@d_why: 英文要先想清楚两件事：这件事是在说**结果、经历、到现在为止**，还是在说**过去具体的某一次**？只说结果和经历、不提具体时间 → **have done**（I've tried stinky tofu. D32；Shenzhen has changed a lot. D36）；一出现具体的过去时间（yesterday / on Thursday / last year / ago）→ **过去式**（Prices went up last year. D36）。**just / already / yet** 是完成时的三个好帮手（D33）：just「刚」、already「已经」放在 have 和动词中间；yet 放句尾，只用在疑问句和否定句里。美式口语里 just / already 也常配过去式（Grace just got engaged!），但「完成时 + 具体过去时间」永远是错的。今天林晓说 I've just passed my probation!——没提时间，所以用完成时；要是加上 on Thursday，就得改成 I passed it on Thursday.。讲一次旅行也一样（D57）：经过用过去式，经历用 I've never seen…。
@d_when: 报喜、说经历、问「……了吗」时用 have done + just / already / yet；一说「哪天、哪年、多久以前」就换成过去式。
@d_vs: **I've just passed my probation!** 我刚通过试用期！（报喜，不提时间）\n**I passed it on Thursday.** 我是周四通过的。（有具体时间）\n**Have you ever climbed Luofu Mountain?** — **Yes, I have. I climbed it last weekend.**\n**I've already told Mom.** 我已经告诉妈妈了。／**I haven't told Mei yet.** 我还没告诉小梅。\n✗ I've passed it on Thursday. ✗ I've ever climbed it. ✗ I've told Mei already yesterday.
@d_task: 用英文说：① 我刚搬家了！② 我是上周六搬的。③ 你交房租了吗？——还没。④ 我以前从来没一个人住过。 | ① I've just moved! ② I moved last Saturday. ③ Have you paid the rent yet? — Not yet. ④ I've never lived alone before.
@diff: 「两年了」「从初中起」「两个月前」——for / since / ago（复习 D31、D34、D36、D58）
@d_cn: 中文都带着时间，「十年了」「从初中起」「两个月前」，却不在动词上区分「一段时间」「起点」和「过去的一个时间点」。
@d_en: We've known each other for ten years. / We've known each other since middle school. / Two months ago, I joined SwiftLink.
@d_why: **for + 一段时间**（ten years / two months / ages），**since + 起点**（middle school / 2020 / I moved here），两个都配 **have done / have been doing**，表示「一直持续到现在」（D31、D34）；**ago** 跟在一段时间**后面**，表示「从现在往回数的那个时间点」，配**过去式**（D36）。中文「我在深圳住了两年了」最容易说成 ✗ I live in Shenzhen two years.——缺了完成时，也缺了 for（D31）。另外三个常见错误：✗ since two years（since 后面要起点）；✗ I've joined SwiftLink two months ago（有 ago 就是具体时间点，要用过去式）；回答 How long have you…? 时，口语里只说 For two years. / Since 2023. 就够了；说完整句子别漏掉 for：I've lived here for two years.（D31）。今天林晓说 We've known each other since middle school（起点），又说 Two months ago, I was just like you（时间点）。
@d_when: 问和答「多久了」：How long have you…? + for / since；说「……之前发生了什么」：一段时间 + ago + 过去式。
@d_vs: **We've known each other for ten years.** 我们认识十年了。\n**We've known each other since middle school.** 我们从初中起就认识。\n**We met ten years ago.** 我们是十年前认识的。\n**I've walked to work since I moved.** ／ **I moved three weeks ago.**\n✗ We've known each other since ten years. ✗ We have met ten years ago.
@d_task: 用英文说：① 我在这家公司工作两个月了。② 我搬家以后，一直走路上班。③ 小梅是上周日去上海的。④ 我三年前搬到深圳。 | ① I've worked at this company for two months. ② I've walked to work since I moved.（或 I've been walking to work since I moved.）③ Mei left for Shanghai last Sunday. ④ I moved to Shenzhen three years ago.
