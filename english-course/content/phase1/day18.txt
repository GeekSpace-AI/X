@culture_title: 委婉地指出客户的错误
@culture: 发现客户的错误一定要指出来（否则做错了算谁的？），但说法很重要。不要说 You made a mistake! / Your PO is wrong.（听起来像在指责）。
@culture: 美式说法是**说事实 + 请对方确认**：I noticed that the color on the PO says white. Could you double-check that? / Just to confirm, did you mean silver? 把「错」留给对方自己发现，对方会很感激：Good catch! Thanks for pointing that out.
@culture: 另外，中国习惯合同盖公章，美国公司一般**只签字**，邮件确认的 PO 本身就有法律效力。所以 PO 上的每一个字都要认真核对。

@think_title: 「如果……就……」：英文只要一个 if
@think_intro: 中文「如果……就……」是成对出现的，英文的 if 只要一个就够了：If you order more, **we'll** lower the price. 后半句**不需要 so**，也很少用 then。
@think_intro: 最重要的规则：**if 后面说将来的事，用现在时**：If it **rains** tomorrow（不是 will rain）。后半句才用 will。
@think_intro: 再加三个好用的「条件词」：只要 **as long as**；除非 **unless**；以防万一 **in case**。谈判、确认订单时天天用得上。
@pair: 如果你订 3,000 台，我们就降价。 | If you order 3,000 units, so we lower the price. | If you order 3,000 units, we'll lower the price. | if 句后面不加 so
@pair: 如果明天下雨，我们就不去了。 | If tomorrow will rain, we don't go. | If it rains tomorrow, we won't go. | if 后面用现在时说将来
@pair: 只要定金到了，我们就开工。 | Only deposit arrive, we start. | As long as the deposit arrives, we'll start production. | 「只要」→ as long as
@pair: 除非出意外，我们 12 月出货。 | Except accident, we ship December. | Unless something goes wrong, we'll ship in December. | 「除非」→ unless
@pair: 带把伞，以防下雨。 | Take umbrella, maybe rain. | Take an umbrella in case it rains. | 「以防万一」→ in case
@pair: 有问题就告诉我。 | Have problem, tell me. | If there's any problem, just let me know. | if + there's
@pair: 你不确认，我们就没法开工。 | You no confirm, we can't start. | We can't start unless you confirm. | 也可以说 If you don't confirm, …
@pair: 如果我是你，我会先要样品。 | If I am you, I will ask sample. | If I were you, I'd ask for samples first. | 给建议的固定句型
@think_tip: 「如果 A，就 B」→ **If + A（现在时）, B（will）**，不加 so；「只要」→ **as long as**；「除非」→ **unless**；「以防万一」→ **in case**；给建议 → **If I were you, I'd…**

@pron_title: 意群停顿：长句子要「切块」读
@pron: 长句子不要一口气读完，也不要一个词一个词地停。要按**意思**切成几块（意群），块与块之间稍微停一下，块内部连起来读。
@pron: 例：As long as we receive the deposit / by next Friday, / we can ship / on December fifteenth.
@pron: 一般在逗号、if / as long as / unless 这类连接词前、以及长主语后面停顿。**不要**在 the、a、to 后面停顿。
@pron_drill: 切块朗读：If you send me a revised PO today, / I'll send you the PI tomorrow. ｜ Unless something goes wrong, / we'll ship in December. ｜ Take an umbrella / in case it rains.
