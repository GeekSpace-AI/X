# 第三册编写计划（Day 61–90）

第三册的编写蓝图：每天的场景、中英差异点、指定新词、指定短语和锚点句。`build.py` 只读取 `day*.txt`，本文件不进入 PDF。

## 通用规则

- **新词不重复**：第一、二册已注释的词和短语（`python3 build.py check` 会自动比对）在第三册**不再注释**，只在新语境中自然复现。下表中每个词 / 短语只在指定的那一天注释。
- **锚点句**：每天 5 个锚点句必须**原样**出现在当天的【C】里。第二天的热身从前一天的锚点句取 3 句，从 3–7 天前取 1 句，**再从第一、二册各取 1 句**（重点复现第二册的薄弱项：完成时 vs 过去时、for / since、used to / be used to、讲故事时态一致、has / have）。
- **文本**：第三册的【C】可以是对话、邮件、聊天记录、报告或几种混合；邮件和报告的句子不加说话人，前面用一句叙述交代（如 Lily writes to Sarah:）。长度：D61–66 约 220–240 词；D68–73 约 230–250；D75–80 约 240–260；D82–89 约 250–280；复习日约 240–280。
- **听力**：第三册四步听法为「盲听两遍 → 看英文听 → 难句看中文 → 段落听写」，**【I】的前 2 题是听力题**（只听不看就能回答）。
- **输出**：今日输出交替进行：口头汇报 1.5–2 分钟，或写一封 6–10 句的工作邮件 / 消息，都附示范。
- **人物**：林晓 Lily（已转正，独立负责 20 个美国卖家）、张涛 Tom（经理，要求「先说结论」）、Emma（产品经理，管功能和路线图）、吴佳 Grace（客服，熟悉退款和赔偿规则）、李凯 Kevin（IT，管账号权限）、Mike（西雅图工程经理，管系统和 API）、实习生 Leo（林晓带他）。卖家：**Sarah Miller**（俄亥俄州家居网店 Pine & Pebble 的老板，友好）、**Daniel Ross**（得州球鞋店 Ross Kicks 的老板，要求多、脾气急）、**Priya Nair**（大卖家 BrightNest 的运营经理，做事讲数据）。外部角色在故事里用名字作说话人。
- **补充资源**（`@res`，可选）：只写「在 YouTube 频道 Rachel's English 内搜索 "……"」或「在 youglish.com 输入 "……"，选 US」，**不编造任何具体视频链接**。

## 每天的安排

### Day 61 · 我负责什么 / What I'm Responsible For（第 1 周）
- 场景：林晓转正后第一个周一，张涛让她独立负责 20 个美国卖家；实习生 Leo 问她新工作具体做什么。
- 中英差异：①「我负责……」I'm responsible for + 名词 / -ing（不说 I responsible…）②「我的工作是……」My job is to… / My main job is -ing ③「主要」main（形容词）vs mainly（副词）④「我顶替她」I'm covering for her / I'm taking over
- 新词（20）：duty, merchant, platform, region, assign, own, mainly, priority, coordinate, handover, onboarding, monitor, regular, escalate, inbox, portfolio, overview, involve, cover, accountable
- 短语（5）：be responsible for, point of contact, account manager, on a daily basis, take over
- 锚点句：I'm responsible for twenty sellers in the U.S. ｜ My main job is to keep them happy. ｜ I'm their point of contact. ｜ I check the dashboard on a daily basis. ｜ If there's a big problem, I escalate it to Tom.

### Day 62 · 一步一步来 / Step by Step（第 1 周）
- 场景：林晓教 Leo 给新卖家开通账号的流程：先验证资料、再上传模板、然后配置设置、最后激活。
- 中英差异：① 中文「先……然后……再……最后」→ To begin with / Next / Then / Once that's done / Finally，常配祈使句 ②「上传」upload sth to…；「提交」submit ③「需要……」It requires… / You need to…（不说 It needs you to…）④ once + 一般现在时 =「一……就……」
- 新词（20）：process, stage, procedure, require, approve, approval, verify, upload, submit, template, checklist, manual, instruction, screenshot, folder, tool, setting, configure, activate, sequence
- 短语（5）：to begin with, once that's done, step by step, go through, send over
- 锚点句：To begin with, we verify the seller's information. ｜ Next, upload the product template to the system. ｜ Once that's done, the account needs Tom's approval. ｜ Let's go through it step by step. ｜ Finally, we activate the account.

### Day 63 · 订单已经发出 / The Order Has Been Shipped（第 1 周）

- 中英差异：①「订单发出去了」The order has been shipped.（东西做主语 → 被动；中文不说「被」）② 被动结构 be + 过去分词，时态变在 be 上：is / was / has been / will be ③「系统自动生成」It's generated automatically. ④ 需要说出谁做的才加 by…
- 新词（20）：lifecycle, generate, create, collect, sort, transport, unload, notify, notification, automatically, manually, dispatch, fulfill, fulfillment, inventory, item, batch, pallet, supplier, flow
- 短语（5）：place an order, by hand, in bulk, on behalf of, pass on
- 锚点句：Once an order is placed, a shipping label is created automatically. ｜ The package is picked up from your warehouse. ｜ It's sorted and loaded onto a truck. ｜ Your customer is notified at every step. ｜ The order has been shipped.

### Day 65 · 写一封清楚的工作邮件 / Writing a Clear Work Email（第 1 周）
- 场景：张涛退回林晓写的一封长邮件：「先说目的」；林晓重写，Grace 帮她看语气。
- 中英差异：① 中文邮件常先寒暄、自我介绍；英文开头直接说目的：I'm writing to… ②「麻烦您……」Could you please…?（不说 Trouble you…）③「如下」Please see below. / as follows ④ 结尾：Thanks, / Best regards,（不堆砌客套）
- 新词（20）：purpose, regarding, following, below, above, summary, point, clarify, mention, draft, reference, recipient, sender, greeting, paragraph, bullet, tone, brief, concise, action
- 短语（5）：I'm writing to…, Please see below, at your earliest convenience, as mentioned, Best regards
- 锚点句：I'm writing to confirm the new delivery times. ｜ Please see below for the details. ｜ As mentioned in my last email, the change starts on Monday. ｜ Could you please reply at your earliest convenience? ｜ Best regards, Lily

### Day 66 · 您的意思是……？ / Just to Confirm（第 1 周）
- 场景：Emma 给林晓布置一个新功能的测试任务，要求说得很模糊；林晓用「确认、复述、举例」把需求问清楚。
- 中英差异：①「你的意思是……吗？」Do you mean…? / So what you're saying is…（不说 Your meaning is…）②「我确认一下」Just to confirm, … ③「换句话说」In other words, … ④「我以为……」I thought…（结果不是）vs I assume…（推测）
- 新词（20）：understanding, requirement, assume, misunderstand, unclear, vague, context, meaning, rephrase, summarize, general, literal, field, format, scope, feature, intend, precise, obvious, whether
- 短语（5）：just to confirm, Do you mean…?, in other words, get it right, what you're saying is
- 锚点句：Just to confirm, you need it by Friday? ｜ Do you mean the new search feature? ｜ So what you're saying is the format is wrong. ｜ In other words, we test it before Mike's team releases it. ｜ I want to get it right the first time.

### Day 68 · 读数字 / Numbers in English（第 2 周）
- 场景：月初，张涛让林晓整理上个月的数据：订单量、准时率、平均时效；林晓练习用英语读大数字、小数和百分比。
- 中英差异：① 英文数字三位一组：一亿 = one hundred million；125 万 = one point two five million ② 小数：3.5 = three point five；0.75 = zero point seven five ③「提高了 5 个百分点」five percentage points（不说 five percent）④ 分数：三分之一 one third；四分之三 three quarters
- 新词（20）：percentage, decimal, fraction, third, double, triple, figure, digit, roughly, exact, approximately, nearly, billion, dozen, calculate, ratio, zero, minus, plus, equal
- 短语（5）：out of, on average, in total, at least, up to
- 锚点句：We shipped one point two million packages last month. ｜ On average, a package takes six point five days. ｜ Ninety-four out of a hundred packages arrived on time. ｜ That's roughly a third of our total volume. ｜ The on-time rate went up by two percentage points.

### Day 69 · 涨了还是跌了 / Up or Down?（第 2 周）
- 场景：林晓看 Priya 的店铺数据：订单量上涨、退货率下降；她学着描述趋势，再给 Priya 发一条简短的数据消息。
- 中英差异：① increase by（增加了多少）vs increase to（增加到多少）②「下降」fall / drop / decline 的区别和搭配 ③ 程度副词：sharply / slightly / dramatically ④「同比」year over year；「环比」month over month
- 新词（20）：rise, fall, drop, trend, decline, jump, sharply, dramatically, significantly, stable, remain, fluctuate, previous, period, volume, result, highest, lowest, reduce, boost
- 短语（5）：increase by, increase to, year over year, stay flat, hit a record high
- 锚点句：Orders increased by fifteen percent this month. ｜ They increased to twelve thousand. ｜ The return rate dropped sharply in March. ｜ Delivery times have remained stable. ｜ We hit a record high last week.

### Day 72 · 为什么数字变了 / Why Did It Change?（第 2 周）
- 场景：月中，Daniel 的店铺订单突然下降、配送变慢；林晓查原因（假期、港口罢工、仓库爆仓），向张涛说明因果。
