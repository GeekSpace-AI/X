@diff: 「先……然后……再……最后」——To begin with / Next / Then / Once that's done / Finally
@d_cn: 教别人做一件事，按顺序说：先核实资料，然后上传模板，再配置设置，最后激活账号。
@d_en: To begin with, we verify the seller's information. / Next, upload the product template to the system. / Then we configure the settings. / Once that's done, the account needs Tom's approval. / Finally, we activate the account.
@d_why: 中文讲步骤靠「先、然后、再、最后」，「然后」可以一路用下去；英文一连串 then 听着很单调，讲流程要换着用几个**路标词**：To begin with / First → Next → Then → Once that's done / After that → Finally。每个都放句首，说的时候后面停一下，写的时候加逗号。动词常用**祈使句**（Upload…, Check…）或 **we + 动词**（we verify…），很少说 ✗ You should first…, then you should…。两个大坑：①「首先」不说 ✗ **At first**——at first 是「起初……（后来变了）」：At first, I was nervous.；②「最后（一步）」说 **Finally**，不说 ✗ **At last**（「等了很久，终于」），也不说 ✗ In the end（第二册学过，「结果、到头来」）。
@d_when: 带新人、写操作说明、讲开户 / 报销 / 退货等任何流程。
@d_vs: ✓ **To begin with, we verify the seller's information.** 首先核实卖家资料。\n✓ **Next, upload the product template to the system.** 下一步上传产品模板。\n✓ **Once that's done, the account needs Tom's approval.** 这一步完成后，账号需要张涛审批。\n✓ **Finally, we activate the account.** 最后激活账号。\n✗ At first, we verify… ／ ✗ At last, we activate the account.
@d_task: 用英文讲「订会议室」的三步：先登录系统，然后选时间，最后点 Book。 | To begin with, log in to the system. Next, choose a time. Finally, click "Book."
@diff: 「上传」「提交」——upload sth to… / submit
