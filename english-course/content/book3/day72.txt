@v: cause | = | n. | kɔːz | 原因；起因 | 查问题、解释数据变化 | the cause of…；the main cause；find the cause | We still don't know the cause of the delay. | 我们还不知道延误的原因。 | 「……的原因」说 the cause of / the reason for（第一册），介词别弄混：✗ the cause for / ✗ the reason of；cause 偏「造成问题的根源」，reason 偏「解释、理由」；也能作动词：The storms caused delays.
@v: unusual | = | adj. | ʌnˈjuːʒuəl | 不寻常的；少见的；反常的 | 发现数据异常、情况反常 | unusual for sb；an unusual drop；It's unusual to… | It's unusual for him to be late. | 他很少迟到。 | 中文「反常」「不太对劲」在工作里常说 That's unusual.，比 strange（奇怪，第一册）更中性、更客观；常用句型 It's unusual for sb to do sth；s 读 `/ʒ/`，和 usual 一样。
@v: holiday | = | n. | ˈhɑːlədeɪ | 节日；节假日 | 说大促、假期对订单和物流的影响 | a holiday weekend；holiday sales；the holidays | Many stores run big sales on holiday weekends. | 很多店铺在节假日长周末搞大促。 | 美式英语里 holiday 主要指「法定节日」（a national holiday），自己请的「休假」说 vacation（Day 67）；the holidays（复数）在美国常指感恩节到新年这段时间；a holiday weekend 是因节日多放一天的三天长周末。
@v: led to | lead to | phr. | ˈled tə | 导致；带来（某种结果） | 讲因果链 | lead to a spike；lead to delays；lead to problems | Better photos led to fewer returns. | 产品照片拍得更好以后，退货变少了。 | 「A 导致 B」说 A led to B，to 是介词，后面接名词，不接句子：✗ led to orders went up；过去式是 led `/led/`，不是 ✗ leaded；不说 ✗ made orders increase。
@v: spike | = | n. | spaɪk | （数字）突然猛增；尖峰 | 说订单、流量、投诉突然暴涨 | a spike in orders；a sudden spike；a spike in traffic | We saw a spike in website traffic on Monday. | 周一网站流量突然暴涨。 | spike 是「短时间冲高」，像折线图上的一根尖刺；jump（Day 69）是「跳涨」；「……猛增」说 a spike **in** orders，介词用 in；也能作动词：Orders spiked last week.
@v: capacity | = | n. | kəˈpæsəti | 容量；处理能力；产能 | 仓库、系统、团队能处理多少 | warehouse capacity；at full capacity；have the capacity for… | Our system doesn't have the capacity for that much traffic. | 我们的系统扛不住那么大的流量。 | 中文「产能」「处理能力」「运力」都可以用 capacity；「满负荷」说 at full capacity；不说 ✗ The warehouse's ability is not enough.；重音在 pa：ca-PA-ci-ty。

@v: due to | = | phr. | ˈduː tə | 由于；因为 | 说原因，常见于报告、通知、正式邮件 | due to the strike；due to bad weather；due to a system problem | The flight was canceled due to bad weather. | 航班因天气恶劣取消了。 | due to 后面接名词，不接句子：✓ due to the strike ✗ due to there was a strike；比 because of 正式；due 美式读 `/duː/`；due 单用是「到期」：The report is due Friday.
@v: partly | = | adv. | ˈpɑːrtli | 部分地；在一定程度上 | 说一个原因只占一部分 | partly because…；partly due to…；partly caused by… | The drop was partly because of the weather. | 下降有一部分是天气原因。 | partly（部分地）和 largely / mainly（主要地）常成对出现：The delay was largely due to the strike and partly due to the weather.；不说 ✗ part caused by。

@v: seasonal | = | adj. | ˈsiːzənl | 季节性的 | 说每年固定时候的涨落 | seasonal demand；a seasonal drop；seasonal workers | Our business is very seasonal. | 我们的生意季节性很强。 | 说数据时，seasonal 指「每年这个时候本来就会这样」：大促后订单回落、冬天外套好卖；两个 s 都读 `/z/`：SEA-zon-al。

@diff: 「因为」——because + 句子 vs because of / due to + 名词
@d_cn: 「因为港口罢工，配送变慢了」「因为天气不好」——中文「因为」后面跟词、跟句子都行。
@d_en: Delivery times went up due to the strike at the port. / Because of the delays, he got nine one-star reviews. / Deliveries were slow because there was a strike.
@d_why: 中文「因为」后面可以是一个词（因为天气），也可以是一句话（因为港口罢工了）。英文要分开：**because + 完整句子**（有主语和动词）：because there was a strike；**because of / due to + 名词**：because of the strike / due to the strike。最常见的错误是 ✗ because the strike（缺动词）和 ✗ due to there was a strike（due to 后面接了句子）。due to 比 because of 正式，报告、通知里很常见：The flight was delayed due to bad weather. 口语里 because 常弱读成 `/bɪkəz/`，甚至只剩 'cause，听的时候要留意。
@d_when: 解释数据变化、写延误通知、回答 Why…?
@d_vs: ✓ **Delivery times went up due to the strike at the port.** 因为港口罢工，配送时间变长了。（due to + 名词，偏正式）\n✓ **Because of the delays, he got nine one-star reviews.** 因为延误，他收到了九个差评。（because of + 名词）\n✓ **Deliveries were slow because there was a strike.** 配送慢是因为有罢工。（because + 句子）\n✗ because the strike ✗ due to there was a strike
@d_task: 用两种说法说「因为下大雨，包裹晚到了」：一种用 because + 句子，一种用 due to + 名词。 | The packages were late because it rained heavily. ／ The packages were late due to heavy rain.
@diff: 「导致」——lead to / cause / result in（不说 make … happen）
@d_cn: 「节日大促导致订单激增」「暴风雨造成了延误」。
@d_en: The holiday led to a spike in orders. / The storms caused delays. / The strike resulted in a shortage.
@d_why: 中文「导致」「造成」「使得」，学生容易直译成 ✗ The holiday made orders increase a lot. 或 ✗ made the delay happen——不一定算错，但很笨重。英文用一个动词把「原因 → 结果」连起来：**A led to B**（导致，最常用，中性）、**A caused B**（造成，多指问题）、**A resulted in B**（结果是，偏正式）。三个词后面都接**名词**：led to a spike / caused delays / resulted in a shortage。反过来说「B 是 A 造成的」用被动：The delay was partly caused by bad weather.——被动句里的 was 弱读成 `/wəz/`，很容易听漏。
@d_when: 讲因果链、分析数据、写问题说明。
