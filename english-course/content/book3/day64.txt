@diff: 「这个归谁管？」——Who handles this? / Who should I talk to about…?
@d_cn: 一件事不知道该找谁：发票找谁？退款谁负责？登录问题归哪个部门？
@d_en: Who handles invoices? / Do you deal with refunds? / Who should I talk to about a login problem?
@d_why: 中文「归谁管」「找谁」很灵活，直译容易出错：✗ Invoices belong to who?（belong to 是「属于」，说东西归谁所有）、✗ Who manages this invoice?（manage 是「管理」人或业务，用在一张发票上太重）。英文最自然的是 **Who handles…?**（第一册 D4 学过 handle），口语也说 **Who deals with…?**，或者从自己出发问 **Who should I talk to about…?**。别忘了第一册的老问题：**who 做主语时，动词按单数加 -s**：✓ Who handles…? ✗ Who handle…?。问对方本人就说 Do you deal with…? / Do you handle…?
@d_when: 刚接手工作、遇到不熟悉的问题、准备把问题交给别的部门之前。
@d_vs: ✓ **Who handles invoices?** 发票归谁管？\n✓ **Do you deal with refunds?** 退款是你处理吗？\n✓ **Who should I talk to about a login problem?** 登录问题该找谁？\n✗ Who handle invoices? ／ ✗ Invoices belong to who? ／ ✗ Who manages this invoice?
