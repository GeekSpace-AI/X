@diff: increase by（增加了多少）vs increase to（增加到多少）
@d_cn: 订单增长了 15%，增长到了一万二。
@d_en: Orders increased by fifteen percent this month. / They increased to twelve thousand. / Orders increased from 10,400 to 12,000.
@d_why: 中文靠「了」和「到」区分：「增长了 15%」说变化的幅度，「增长到一万二」说新的数值。英文靠介词：**by + 变化量**，**to + 新数值**，**from + 原来的数值**，三个可以连用：Orders increased **from** 10,400 **to** 12,000, or **by** fifteen percent. Leo 说 ✗ They increased to fifteen percent，意思就成了「增长到了 15%」，好像订单量本身是个百分比。下降也一样：fell **by** four points / fell **to** five percent。名词用法：an increase **of** fifteen percent（增幅）/ an increase **in** orders（哪方面增加）。口语里 by 有时会省掉（Orders increased fifteen percent），但 to 绝不能乱用。increase 作动词重音在后 in-CREASE，作名词重音在前 IN-crease。
@d_when: 汇报数据变化、写周报月报、讲图表时。
@d_vs: ✓ **Orders increased by fifteen percent.** 订单增长了 15%。\n✓ **They increased to twelve thousand.** 增长到了一万二。\n✓ **Orders increased from 10,400 to 12,000.**\n✓ **an increase of fifteen percent / an increase in orders**\n✗ Orders increased to fifteen percent.（变成了「增长到 15%」）
@d_task: 用两句话说：退货率从 9% 降到 5%，降了 4 个百分点。 | The return rate fell from nine percent to five percent. It fell by four percentage points.
@diff: 「下降」——fall / drop / decline，「让它下降」reduce
@d_cn: 订单下降、退货率下降、成本降低……中文一个「下降」「降低」就够了。
@d_en: The return rate dropped sharply in March. / It fell from nine to five percent. / Your return rate has declined significantly. / That reduced returns dramatically.
@d_why: 英文有好几个词，区别在语气和速度：**fall**（fell, fallen）最中性、最常用，后面常接 from…to…；**drop** 口语常用，常暗示**快、突然**，和 sharply 是好搭档；**decline** 比较正式，多用在报告里，常指**逐渐、持续**地下降。这三个说数据时都是**不及物**的：主语是「下降的东西」——The return rate dropped.。要说「**某人 / 某事让它下降**」，用及物动词 **reduce**：The photos **reduced** returns.，不说 ✗ The return rate reduced.。上升也分两类：**rise / go up / increase / jump**（自己涨）vs **boost / raise**（让它涨）。rise 和 raise 最容易混：The rate **rose**.（自己升）／ We **raised** prices.（我们提价；raise 也是 D59 的「加薪」）。口头汇报时，第二册学过的 go up / go down（D36）仍然最常用。
@d_when: 报告和消息用 decline / fall，口头汇报用 drop / go down；说原因和措施用 reduce / boost。
@d_vs: **The return rate dropped sharply.** 退货率骤降。（快）\n**It fell from nine to five percent.** 从 9% 降到 5%。（中性）\n**Complaints have declined steadily.** 投诉在逐步减少。（正式、渐进）\n**The photos reduced returns.** 图片让退货减少了。（及物：谁让它降）\n✗ The return rate reduced. / ✗ Orders were risen.
@d_task: 说两句话：① 过去三个月投诉逐渐减少（正式）② 新的尺码表让退货大大减少。 | ① Complaints have declined over the past three months. ② The new size chart reduced returns dramatically.
@diff: 程度副词——sharply / slightly / dramatically / significantly
@d_cn: 「大幅下降」「略有上升」「显著减少」「猛涨」。
@d_en: The return rate dropped sharply. / That reduced returns dramatically. / Your return rate has declined significantly. / Orders rose slightly.
@d_why: 中文把程度放在动词前：「大幅下降」；英文的程度副词一般放在**动词后面**：dropped **sharply**、rose **slightly**（D54）。几个常用词：**sharply** 快而陡，像图上一条很陡的线；**dramatically** 变化非常大，带一点「惊人」的感觉，别滥用；**significantly**「明显、显著」，报告里最稳妥；**slightly**「略微」。中国学习者常说 ✗ increased very much / ✗ decreased a lot of，或把形容词当副词用：✗ dropped sharp。记住两套结构：**动词 + 副词**（dropped sharply）和**形容词 + 名词**（a sharp drop），意思一样，第二种在报告里很常见：There was a sharp drop in returns in March.
@d_when: 描述图表和趋势：口头汇报、周报、给客户的数据消息。
@d_vs: **dropped sharply = a sharp drop** 骤降\n**rose slightly = a slight rise** 小幅上升\n**declined significantly = a significant decline** 明显下降\n**changed dramatically = a dramatic change** 巨大变化\n✗ increased very much / ✗ dropped sharp

@diff: 「同比」year over year；「环比」month over month
@d_cn: 订单同比增长 40%，环比增长 15%。
@d_en: Year over year, orders are up forty percent. / Order volume rose 15% from the previous month. / Month over month, orders are up fifteen percent.
@d_why: 中文财经报道里「同比」「环比」用得很多，学生常直译成 ✗ same ratio / ✗ ring ratio，美国人完全听不懂。**同比**＝和去年同一时期比：**year over year**（缩写 YoY），或说 compared with the same period last year；**环比**＝和上一个时期比：**month over month**（MoM）、week over week，或者用大白话 from the previous month / compared with last month。year over year 可以放句首当副词（Year over year, orders are up 40%.），放在名词前要加连字符：year-over-year growth。一个数字最好说清楚**和谁比**：Orders rose 15% from the previous month and 40% year over year.——只说 Orders rose 15%，Priya 这样讲数据的客户马上会追问：Compared with what?
@d_when: 月报、季报、和卖家聊数据；读美国公司的财报新闻。
@d_vs: **同比：year over year (YoY) = compared with the same period last year**\n**环比：month over month (MoM) = from the previous month**\n✓ **Year over year, orders are up forty percent.**\n✓ **year-over-year growth**（作定语，加连字符）\n✗ same ratio / ✗ ring ratio
