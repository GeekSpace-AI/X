@diff: 「我本来打算……」——I was going to…, but…
@d_cn: 说出原来的计划，以及计划为什么没实现。
@d_en: I was going to finish it this morning, but the system was down.
@d_why: 中文靠「本来」两个字就说清了「计划没实现」。很多人会说 I originally wanted to…，wanted to 只说「想」，听不出那是已经定好的计划。I planned to… 也对，但口语里美国人最常用的是 **was / were going to**：把 Day 16 学的 be going to（已决定的计划）放到过去，意思就是「当时的计划是……」，后面几乎总跟着 **but + 原因**。
@d_when: 解释工作没按时完成、计划被打乱、临时改了主意：I was going to call you, but I forgot.
@d_vs: **I was going to finish it this morning, but the system was down.** 我本来打算上午做完，但是系统宕机了。\n**I'm going to finish it tomorrow.** 我明天会做完。（现在的计划）\n✗ I originally will finish it this morning.（will 不能这样说过去）
@d_task: 用英文说：「我本来打算昨天给你打电话的，但是我忘了。」 | I was going to call you yesterday, but I forgot.
@diff: 「应该今天到的」——was supposed to
@d_cn: 按照安排、约定或规定，事情「本来应该」发生（但实际上没有）。
@d_en: The samples were supposed to arrive today. / I'm supposed to send the report by Friday.
@d_why: 中文的「应该」有好几个意思：建议（你应该休息）、推测（他应该到了）、按安排本该如此（应该今天到的）。英文把它们分开：建议用 should（Day 24 详细讲），**按安排、按计划本该如此**用 **be supposed to**。说成过去时 was / were supposed to，几乎就等于「本来应该……结果没有」。如果说 The samples should arrive today.，美国人听到的是「样品今天应该会到」（推测），听不出「没到」。
@d_when: 说延误、和计划不一致的情况：The meeting was supposed to start at two.；也能说现在的安排或要求：I'm supposed to meet Emma at three.
@d_vs: **They were supposed to arrive today.** 本来应该今天到。（结果没到）\n**They should arrive today.** 今天应该会到。（推测）\n**I'm supposed to send it by Friday.** 按要求，我周五前要发出去。
@d_task: 用英文说：「会议本来应该三点开始，但是 Tom 迟到了。」 | The meeting was supposed to start at three, but Tom was late.
@diff: 准点 on time ≠ 来得及 in time
@d_cn: 「准时、按时」和「赶得上、来得及」是两个不同的意思。
@d_en: The samples didn't arrive on time. / I'll still get it in time for my meeting.
@d_why: 中文「准时」「按时」「及时」都带一个「时」，英文只差一个介词，意思却不同：**on time** = 按预定的时间点，不早不晚（准点）；**in time** = 在某件事之前、还没太晚（来得及），常说 in time for + 事情。中国学生常把两种意思都说成 on time。
@d_when: 说航班、会议、交货是否准点 → on time；说赶不赶得上某件事 → in time (for…)；刚好赶上 → just in time
@d_vs: **The samples didn't arrive on time.** 样品没有按时到。\n**I'll still get it in time for my meeting.** 开会前还来得及拿到。\n**We got to the station just in time.** 我们刚好赶上。
@d_task: 选 on time 或 in time 说：① 会议准时开始了。② 你明天上午发给我，开会前还来得及。 | ① The meeting started on time. ② If you send it tomorrow morning, I'll get it in time for the meeting.
@diff: 「是我的错」——It's my fault.
@d_cn: 主动承认问题是自己造成的。
@d_en: Sorry, it's my fault. / That was my mistake.
@d_why: 很多人直译成 It's my wrong. 或 I'm wrong.——wrong 是形容词，不能放在 my 后面当名词；I'm wrong 是「我（说 / 想）错了」，指观点、判断。「责任在我」用 **fault**；「我弄错了」用 **mistake**。在美国职场，简短、直接地认错，马上说出补救办法，会被看作专业、可靠；不用一遍遍道歉，也不用先讲一长串理由。
@d_when: 自己漏了东西、弄错了信息、造成了延误；别人向你道歉时安慰对方：It's not your fault.
@d_vs: **Sorry, it's my fault.** 抱歉，是我的错。\n**That was my mistake. I'll fix it.** 是我弄错了，我来改。\n**It's not your fault.** 不怪你。\n✗ It's my wrong.
@d_task: 你把卖家的地址写错了，向经理承认，并说你马上改。 | Sorry, it's my fault. I wrote the wrong address. I'll fix it right now.

@core: I was going to…, but… | 说明原计划和没做成的原因 | finish it / call you / send it + this morning / yesterday | 正式：I had planned to finish it this morning, but…｜随意：I was gonna do it, but…
@core: Can I send it to you + 新时间? | 主动提出新的交付时间 | tomorrow morning / by noon / on Thursday | 正式：Would it be OK if I sent it tomorrow morning?｜随意：Tomorrow morning OK?
@core: … was / were supposed to + 动作. | 说本该发生却没发生的事 | arrive today / start at two / be ready | 正式：The shipment was scheduled to arrive today.｜随意：It was supposed to be here today.
@core: They're stuck in + 地方. | 说东西或人卡在某个环节 | customs / the warehouse / traffic | 正式：The shipment is being held in customs.｜随意：They're stuck in customs.
@core: Sorry, it's my fault. | 承认是自己的问题 | fault → mistake | 正式：I apologize. That was my mistake.｜随意：My bad.
@core: We expect … to + 动作. | 说预计的时间或结果 | clear customs / arrive / be ready | 正式：We expect them to clear customs on Thursday.｜随意：Should be Thursday.
@core: I'm a little behind schedule. | 说进度落后 | a little / a bit / two days | 正式：We're about two days behind schedule.｜随意：I'm running a bit late on it.
