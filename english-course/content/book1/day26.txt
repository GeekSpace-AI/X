@v: quality | = | n. | ˈkwɑːləti | 质量；品质 | 评价产品和服务 | good quality；high quality；service quality | This printer paper is good quality. | 这种打印纸质量很好。 | quality 本身不带「好」的意思，「质量好」要说 good / high quality，「质量差」说 poor quality；别和长得很像的 quantity（数量）混：quality 管「好不好」，quantity 管「多不多」。
@v: same | = | adj. | seɪm | 相同的；一样的 | 说两样东西一样或差不多 | the same；about the same；the same as… | My phone is the same as yours. | 我的手机和你的一样。 | same 前面几乎总要加 the：✗ They are same. → ✓ They're the same.；「和……一样」用 the same as，不说 ✗ same with。

@diff: 「A 比 B 便宜」——形容词加 -er，再用 than
@d_cn: 拿两样东西比较，说其中一个「更……」。
@d_en: A is cheaper than B. / C is faster than A. / Option A is cheaper, but it's slower.
@d_why: 中文把「比 B」放在形容词前面，形容词本身不变：A 比 B 便宜。英文正好反过来：先把形容词变个样（cheap → cheap**er**），再用 **than** 把比较的对象放到后面。照中文语序说 A than B cheap，或者漏掉 -er 说 A is cheap than B，美国人都要愣一下。如果对方已经知道在和谁比，than 后面那部分可以直接省掉。
@d_when: 一个音节的短形容词（cheap / fast / slow / light / big）和以 -y 结尾的词（easy → easier，busy → busier）加 -er；比价格、速度、大小、重量时最常用。
@d_vs: **A is cheaper than B.** A 比 B 便宜。\n✗ A than B cheap. ✗ A is cheap than B.\n**Option A is cheaper, but it's slower.**（大家都知道在和别的方案比，省掉 than）\n**This box is bigger.**（big → bigger，要双写 g）
@d_task: 用英文说：① 这个箱子比那个轻。② C 比 A 快多了。 | ① This box is lighter than that one. ② C is much faster than A. / C is a lot faster than A.
@diff: 「更贵」——more expensive，不是 expensiver
@d_cn: 用比较长的形容词说「更……」「最……」。
@d_en: B is more reliable than A. / C is the most expensive.
@d_why: 中文的「更」「最」可以放在任何形容词前面；英文要看形容词长短：短词加 **-er / -est**，长词（大多数两个音节以上的词）前面加 **more / the most**。两种办法只能选一种，中国学生最常见的错误是「上双保险」：more cheaper、the most fastest。另外有几个最常用的词不按规则变：good → better → best，bad → worse → worst，要单独记住。
@d_when: expensive / reliable / important / interesting / difficult 这类长词用 more / the most；三个或更多选项里选「第一名」用 the -est / the most。
@d_vs: cheap → **cheaper** → **the cheapest**\nexpensive → **more expensive** → **the most expensive**\ngood → **better** → **the best**；bad → **worse** → **the worst**\n✗ more cheaper ✗ the most fastest ✗ more good
@d_task: 用英文说：① B 比 A 可靠。② C 是三个里面最贵的。③ 这个方案比那个好。 | ① B is more reliable than A. ② C is the most expensive of the three. ③ This option is better than that one.
@diff: 「我更喜欢 B」——I prefer B (to C).
@d_cn: 在几个选项里，说自己更倾向哪一个。
@d_en: I prefer B. / I prefer B to C. / I like B better.
@d_why: 中文是「更 + 喜欢」两个词；英文的 **prefer** 一个词就包含了「更喜欢」，所以前面不要再加 more：✗ I more prefer B。要说「和谁比」，用 **to** 而不是 than：I prefer B to C。觉得 prefer 不好用时，口语也可以说 I like B better (than C)。工作中给建议时，美国同事还常说 I'd go with B.（要是我就选 B），或者 I recommend B.，听起来更像在给专业意见。
@d_when: 别人问 Which one do you prefer? / Which one do you like? 时回答；开会时说出自己的倾向，最好紧接着给一个理由：It's more reliable. / It's cheaper.
@d_vs: **I prefer B.** 我更倾向 B。\n**I prefer B to C.** 比起 C，我更喜欢 B。\n**I like B better than C.**（口语，意思同上）\n✗ I more prefer B. ✗ I prefer B than C.
@d_task: 同事问你 Coffee or tea?，你想说「我更喜欢茶，谢谢」，再说一遍「比起咖啡我更喜欢茶」。 | I prefer tea, thanks. / I prefer tea to coffee.
@diff: 「差不多」——about the same / similar / almost
@d_cn: 两样东西几乎一样；或者事情快做完了；或者数字大约是多少。
@d_en: These two are about the same. / Their prices are similar. / It's almost done.
@d_why: 中文一个「差不多」包办了好几种意思：价格差不多、做得差不多了、差不多五点了。英文要按意思分开说：几乎一样 → **about the same**；接近、有点像 → **similar**；快完成了 → **almost**（Day 22 学过 It's almost done）；数字、时间大约 → **about / around**。另外 same 前面几乎总要带 the。
@d_when: 比较价格、质量、大小用 about the same / similar；汇报进度用 almost；说时间、数量用 about / around。
@d_vs: **These two are about the same.** 这两个差不多（几乎一样）。\n**Their prices are similar.** 两家价格差不多（很接近）。\n**It's almost done.** 差不多做完了。\n**It's about five o'clock.** 差不多五点了。\n✗ They are same. → ✓ They're the same.
@d_task: 用英文说：① 这两个箱子大小差不多。② 报告差不多写完了。 | ① These two boxes are about the same size. ② The report is almost done.

@core: A is + 形容词-er + than B. | 比较两样东西（短形容词） | cheaper / faster / lighter / slower | 正式：Option A is less expensive than Option B.｜随意：A's cheaper.
@core: A is more + 形容词 + than B. | 比较两样东西（长形容词） | expensive / reliable / important | 正式：Option B is significantly more reliable than A.｜随意：B's way more reliable.
@core: C is the fastest / the most expensive. | 在三个或更多选项里说「最……」 | the cheapest / the best / the most reliable | 正式：Option C is the fastest of the three.｜随意：C's the fastest.
@core: B isn't as + 形容词 + as A, but it's… | 先承认缺点，再说优点，语气委婉 | cheap / fast / light | 正式：B is not as inexpensive as A, but it is more reliable.｜随意：B's not as cheap, but it's better.
@core: Which one do you prefer? | 请对方在几个选项里做选择 | prefer / like / recommend | 正式：Which option would you prefer?｜随意：Which one do you like?
@core: I prefer B. / I recommend B. | 说出自己的选择或建议，最好再加一个理由 | B / the second one / Option B | 正式：I would recommend Option B.｜随意：I'd go with B.
@core: These two are about the same. | 说两个选项差不多 | about the same / similar / pretty much the same | 正式：The two options are very similar.｜随意：They're pretty much the same.
