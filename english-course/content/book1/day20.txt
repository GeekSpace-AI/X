@diff: 「多少」——How many 还是 How much？
@d_cn: 问数量：「要几箱纸？」「要多少胶带？」「多少钱？」
@d_en: How many boxes do we need? / How much tape do we need? / How much is it?
@d_why: 中文一个「多少」，后面什么都能接；英文要先看后面的东西**能不能一个一个地数**：能数的（boxes、packages、people）用 **How many + 复数**；不能直接数的（tape、paper、water、time）用 **How much**。问价格永远用 **How much**，因为钱在这里是「金额」，不是一个个的东西。
@d_when: 下单、盘点、问人数、问价格。拿不准时，看这个英文名词能不能加 s：boxes 能 → many；tape 不能 → much。
@d_vs: **How many boxes do we need?** 要几箱？（box 可数）\n**How much tape do we need?** 要多少胶带？（tape 不可数）\n**How much is it?** 多少钱？\n✗ How many money? → ✓ How much money?
@d_task: 用 How many 或 How much 问：① 我们有多少个客户？② 打印纸还剩多少？③ 这个多少钱？ | ① How many customers do we have? ② How much printer paper is left? ③ How much is this? / How much does this cost?
@diff: 不可数名词：some advice / some information，不加 s
@d_cn: 「给我一些建议」「一些信息」「一些胶带」。
@d_en: I need some advice. / Here's some information. / a piece of advice / a roll of tape
@d_why: 中文名词没有单复数，「一些建议」「几条信息」都很自然；英文里 **advice、information、paper（纸张）、tape** 这类词被看成「一团」而不是「一个个」，是**不可数名词**：不加 s，前面也不用 a / an。要数它们，就借一个「量词」：**a piece of advice、two pieces of information、a box of paper、a roll of tape**——这和中文「一条建议」「一卷胶带」的思路其实很像。
@d_when: 请别人给建议、提供信息、说办公用品的数量。
@d_vs: ✗ an advice / advices → ✓ some advice / a piece of advice\n✗ informations → ✓ some information\n**a box of paper / a roll of tape** 一箱纸 / 一卷胶带
@d_task: 用英文说：「我能给你一条建议吗？」「网站上有很多信息。」 | Can I give you a piece of advice? / There's a lot of information on the website.
@diff: 「三万」——thirty thousand（英文三位一组）
@d_cn: 说大数字：三万、三十六万、两千三百五十。
@d_en: thirty thousand (30,000) / three hundred sixty thousand (360,000) / two thousand three hundred and fifty (2,350)
@d_why: 中文四位一组：个、十、百、千、**万**、亿；英文三位一组：thousand（千）、**million**（百万）、billion（十亿）。英文里没有「万」，所以「三万」要先换算成「三十个千」= **thirty thousand**；「十万」= one hundred thousand；「一百万」= one million。最省事的办法是看书写时的逗号：30,000 → 逗号前是 30 → thirty thousand。
@d_when: 说包裹量、销售额、价格、人数的时候。2,350 美国人常读 two thousand three hundred fifty，英式会加 and，两种都能听懂。
@d_vs: 三万 = 30,000 = **thirty thousand**（✗ three ten thousand）\n三十六万 = 360,000 = **three hundred sixty thousand**\n两百万 = 2,000,000 = **two million**\n✗ two thousands → ✓ two thousand
@d_task: 用英文读出：① 5 万 ② 12 万 ③ 150 万 | ① fifty thousand ② one hundred twenty thousand ③ one million five hundred thousand / one and a half million
@diff: 「这个多少钱？」——How much is it? / How much does it cost?
@d_cn: 问价格。
@d_en: How much is it? / How much does it cost? / What's the price?
@d_why: 中文「多少钱」直译成 How much money is it? 语法上说得通，但美国人几乎不这么说——问价格时 money 是多余的，**How much** 本身就是「多少钱」。还有两个常见错：「价格是多少」不说 How much is the price?（price 已经是「价格」了）；「这个要多少钱」用 **cost** 时，主语是东西：It costs eighty yuan.，说成 I cost eighty yuan. 就变成「我值八十块」了。
@d_when: 网购、问同事东西多少钱买的、问报价；问一共多少钱：What's the total?
@d_vs: ✓ **How much is it?** / **How much does it cost?** 这个多少钱？\n✓ **What's the price?** 价格是多少？\n✗ How much money is it?　✗ How much is the price?\n**It costs eighty yuan.** 它卖八十块。（✗ I cost eighty yuan.）
@d_task: 同事新买了一个充电器，你问：「这个多少钱？在哪儿买的？」 | How much was it? Where did you buy it? / How much did it cost?
