@v: badge | = | n. | bædʒ | 工牌；胸牌 | 进办公楼、刷门禁、有访客的时候 | wear a badge；name badge；visitor badge | Please show your badge at the front desk. | 请在前台出示你的工牌。 | 美国公司把挂在胸前、带名字和照片的工牌叫 badge（很多工牌同时也是门禁卡 key card）；「戴工牌」用 wear，不用 take。
@v: have to | = | phr. | ˈhæftə | 必须；不得不 | 口头说明规定；说自己必须做的事 | have to go；has to work；had to wait | I have to leave at five today. | 我今天五点得走。 | she / he 用 has to，过去用 had to；读作 `/ˈhæftə/`、`/ˈhæstə/`，不要一个词一个词地读 have-to。
@v: wear | = | v. | wer | 戴；穿（在身上） | 工牌、口罩、衣服、眼镜 | wear a badge；wear a mask；wear glasses | You don't have to wear a suit here. | 在这里不用穿西装。 | 中文「穿衣服」「戴工牌」「戴眼镜」英文都用 wear（说状态）；put on 是「穿上 / 戴上」这个动作：Put on your badge.
@v: rule | = | n. | ruːl | 规定；规则 | 说公司、团队的规矩 | follow the rules；That's the rule.；break a rule | It's a new rule. Everyone has to follow it. | 这是新规定，每个人都要遵守。 | 「遵守规定」用 follow the rules；obey the rules 也对，但语气更像对孩子、士兵下命令。
@v: security | = | n. | sɪˈkjʊrəti | 安全（防盗、防泄密）；安保 | 信息安全、大楼安保 | security policy；IT security；security guard | Call security if you see a stranger in the office. | 在办公室看到陌生人就叫保安。 | security 指防止被偷、被攻击、被泄密的「安全」；人身、用电、消防的「安全」用 safety：safety rules。
@v: policy | = | n. | ˈpɑːləsi | （公司的）制度；规定；政策 | 公司规定、退货政策、隐私政策 | company policy；security policy；return policy | Sorry, it's company policy. | 抱歉，这是公司的规定。 | 拒绝别人时说 It's company policy. 很常见，比 It's our rule 自然；policy 读 PO·li·cy，不读「破里西」。
@v: should | = | modal v. | ʃʊd | 应该（建议）；（推测）应该会 | 给建议、提醒好的做法 | You should…；You shouldn't…；Should I…? | You should ask Grace. She knows everything. | 你应该去问吴佳，她什么都知道。 | should 后面接动词原形：✗ should to reply / should replied → ✓ should reply；「本来应该（结果没有）」用 was supposed to（Day 23）。
@v: within | = | prep. | wɪˈðɪn | 在……之内（不超过一段时间或范围） | 说回复时限、送达时间 | within 24 hours；within a week；within two days | We'll reply within two business days. | 我们会在两个工作日内回复。 | 「24 小时内回复」用 within 24 hours；in 24 hours 常被理解成「24 小时以后」，说时限时容易产生歧义。
@v: urgent | = | adj. | ˈɜːrdʒənt | 紧急的；急的 | 标记急事、请人优先处理 | an urgent message；It's urgent.；urgent order | Sorry to bother you, but it's urgent. | 抱歉打扰你，但这件事很急。 | 「很急」说 It's urgent.，不说 It's very hurry.；hurry 是动词或名词：I'm in a hurry.
@v: don't have to | = | phr. | ˌdoʊnt ˈhæftə | 不必；不用 | 告诉对方某事不是必须的 | don't have to worry；doesn't have to；didn't have to | You don't have to print it. Just email it to me. | 不用打印，直接发邮件给我就行。 | 意思是「不必」，不是「不准」！「不准」用 must not / can't；she / he 说 doesn't have to。
@v: must | = | modal v. | mʌst | 必须（多用于书面规定和强调） | 公司制度、标牌、正式通知 | must not；must be；All staff must… | All visitors must sign in. | 所有访客必须登记。 | 口语说「必须」更常用 have to；must not 是「禁止」，不是「不必」。
@v: share | = | v. | ʃer | 告诉（别人）；分享；共用 | 说密码、文件、信息能不能给别人 | share a file；share your password；share a desk | Can you share the file with me? | 你能把文件共享给我吗？ | 「和谁分享」要加 with：share it with me；✗ share me the file。
@v: strange | = | adj. | streɪndʒ | 奇怪的；可疑的 | 可疑邮件、陌生链接、奇怪的声音 | a strange email；strange links；That's strange. | That's strange. The system was fine this morning. | 奇怪，今天上午系统还好好的。 | strange 是「奇怪的」；「陌生人」是 stranger，不说 strange people。
@v: link | = | n. | lɪŋk | 链接 | 邮件、消息、网页里的链接 | click a link；send sb a link；the link below | Can you send me the link? | 你能把链接发给我吗？ | 「点链接」最常说 click (on) the link；link 结尾的 /k/ 要发出来，不读成「林」。
@v: ticket | = | n. | ˈtɪkɪt | （IT）工单；问题单 | 向 IT 报问题、申请权限 | open a ticket；IT ticket；submit a ticket | I opened a ticket for my printer problem. | 我为打印机的问题开了一个工单。 | ticket 最常见的意思是「票」；在公司里 IT ticket 是「工单」，「提交工单」说 open / submit a ticket。
@v: private | = | adj. | ˈpraɪvət | 保密的；私人的 | 客户信息、个人信息、私人谈话 | private information；keep sth private；a private room | Please keep this private. | 这件事请保密。 | 「隐私」是名词 privacy；「这是我的隐私」说 That's private.，不说 That's my privacy.
@v: allow | = | v. | əˈlaʊ | 允许 | 说规定允许或不允许什么 | allow sb to…；be not allowed；Is it allowed? | We don't allow food in the meeting room. | 会议室不允许带食物。 | 「不允许」口语常用被动：You're not allowed to…；allow 后面接人再接 to do：allow you to use it。
@v: make sure | = | phr. | ˌmeɪk ˈʃʊr | 一定要；确保 | 叮嘱别人别忘了；自己再确认一下 | make sure you…；make sure (that)…；Let me make sure. | Make sure you save the file. | 一定要记得保存文件。 | 「我确定 / 我肯定」是 I'm sure；「确保、一定要做到」是 make sure，两个别混。
@v: log out | = | phr. | ˌlɑːɡ ˈaʊt | 退出登录 | 下班、离开电脑、用完公用电脑之后 | log out of the system；log out of your account | Don't forget to log out of your email. | 别忘了退出邮箱。 | 和 log in（Day 12）相反；「退出某个系统」用 log out of…；名词写成一个词 logout。
@v: necessary | = | adj. | ˈnesəseri | 必要的；必须的 | 问某事有没有必要；说没必要 | Is that necessary?；if necessary；not necessary | It's not necessary to print the email. | 没必要把邮件打印出来。 | 主语一般是 it / that：It's necessary to…；✗ I'm necessary to… 不对。
@v: follow | = | v. | ˈfɑːloʊ | 遵守（规定）；按照（步骤）；跟着 | 遵守规定、按步骤操作 | follow the rules；follow the steps；follow me | Please follow the steps on the screen. | 请按屏幕上的步骤操作。 | 「遵守规定」= follow the rules，不说 keep the rules；follow up（跟进）是另一个短语（Day 29）。

@diff: 「必须」——口语多用 have to
@d_cn: 表达「必须做」：规定、要求，没有选择。
@d_en: You have to wear your badge. / All staff must wear badges.（书面规定）
@d_why: 中国学生课本里先学 must，所以一说「必须」就是 must。但美国人日常说话更常用 **have to**：它只是说明「这是规定 / 情况要求这样」，语气平和。**must** 更正式、更强硬，多出现在书面规定、标牌、员工手册里；当面对同事说 You must…，有点像在下命令。说自己「不得不」也用 have to：I have to go.
@d_when: 口头告诉同事规定 → You have to…；写在制度、通知、标牌上 → must；说自己必须做的事 → I have to…
@d_vs: **You have to wear your badge.** 工牌必须戴着。（口头说明规定）\n**Visitors must sign in at the front desk.** 访客必须在前台登记。（书面）\n**I have to go. I have a meeting.** 我得走了，有个会。\n**She has to work late.** 她得加班。（she / he 用 has to）
@d_task: 用口语告诉新同事：「每周五必须把发货报告发给 Emma。」 | You have to send the shipment report to Emma every Friday.
@diff: 「不必」don't have to ≠「不准」must not
@d_cn: 「不用、不必」= 可以不做，做也行；「不准、绝对不能」= 禁止。
@d_en: You don't have to work on weekends. / You must not share your password.
@d_why: 中文「必须」的否定有两个：「不必」和「不准」，意思差得很远。很多人以为 don't have to 就是 have to 的反面「不能做」，结果把「周末不用上班」听成了「周末不准上班」。记住：**don't have to = 没有必要**（选择权在你）；**must not / mustn't = 禁止**（没有选择）。口语里说「不准」，美国人也常用 **can't** 或直接说 **Don't…**
@d_when: 告诉别人「不用麻烦」「不是必须的」→ don't have to；说规定禁止的事 → must not（书面、强调）/ can't / Don't…（口语）
@d_vs: **You don't have to work on weekends.** 周末不用上班。（想来也行）\n**You must not share your password.** 不准把密码告诉别人。\n**You can't save customer files on your phone.** 不能把客户文件存到手机里。（口语的「不准」）
@d_task: 用英文说：① 你不用打印这份文件。② 你不准把客户的电话号码告诉别人。 | ① You don't have to print this file. ② You must not share customers' phone numbers.
@diff: should = 建议（语气比 have to 软）
@d_cn: 中文「应该」常见两个意思：①建议：你应该早点回复；②推测：包裹应该到了。
@d_en: You should reply within 24 hours. / It should be there by now.
@d_why: **should** 最常用来给建议：「这样做比较好」，语气比 have to 软得多——不是硬性规定，是经验和提醒。should 也能表示推测（It should be there by now. 现在应该到了），但「本来应该（结果没有）」要用 Day 23 的 **was supposed to**。还有一点：中国学生说「你应该……」时爱加重语气，用 must 或 have to，听起来像在训人；给同事建议，should 就够了，想更客气可以说 Maybe you should…
@d_when: 给新人建议、提醒好的做法 → You should… / You shouldn't…；根据经验推测 → It should arrive today.
@d_vs: **You should reply to customers within 24 hours.** 最好在 24 小时内回复客户。（建议）\n**You shouldn't click strange links.** 奇怪的链接最好别点。\n**The package should be there by now.** 包裹现在应该到了。（推测）\n**It was supposed to arrive yesterday.** 本来应该昨天到。（结果没到）
@d_task: 用 should / shouldn't 给新同事两条建议：早点到公司；别把咖啡放在电脑旁边。 | You should come in a little early. You shouldn't put your coffee next to your computer.
@diff: 「你最好……」≠ You'd better
@d_cn: 中文「你最好……」很多时候只是客气的建议。
@d_en: You should… / It's a good idea to… / You'd better…（带警告：不这样就有麻烦）
@d_why: 课本把 had better 翻成「最好」，于是很多人拿它来客气地提建议：You'd better try this restaurant. 可是在美国人听来，**You'd better…** 是「你最好……，不然有你好受的」，带着提醒加警告、甚至上级对下级的味道。普通建议用 **should** 或 **It's a good idea to…**；只有真有后果（迟到、被罚、出问题）时才用 had better，而且更多用来说自己：**I'd better go.**
@d_when: 说自己得赶紧做某事：I'd better go. My meeting starts in five minutes.；明确提醒对方有后果：You'd better wear your badge, or you can't get in.
@d_vs: **You should try the beef noodles.** 你可以尝尝牛肉面。（友好推荐）\n**You'd better try the beef noodles.** 你最好给我尝尝牛肉面。（很怪，像在威胁）\n**I'd better go now.** 我得赶紧走了。（说自己，很自然）
@d_task: 同事周末想去爬山，你推荐他去梧桐山（Wutong Mountain）。用 should 还是 had better？说出来。 | You should go to Wutong Mountain. The view is amazing.（用 should：这是友好推荐，没有警告的意思）

@core: You have to + 动作. | 口头说明规定、要求 | wear your badge / log out / reply in English | 正式：All employees are required to wear their badges.｜随意：You've got to wear your badge.
@core: You must not + 动作. | 强调禁止（规定、安全） | share your password / save files on your phone | 正式：Employees must not share their passwords.｜随意：Never share your password.
@core: You don't have to + 动作. | 告诉对方不必做 | work on weekends / print it / come in early | 正式：It isn't necessary to come in on weekends.｜随意：No need to come in on weekends.
@core: You should / shouldn't + 动作. | 给建议、提醒好的做法 | reply within 24 hours / click strange links | 正式：We recommend replying within 24 hours.｜随意：Maybe reply the same day.
@core: Make sure you + 动作. | 叮嘱对方一定别忘了 | log out / lock your screen / double-check the address | 正式：Please make sure that you log out.｜随意：Don't forget to log out.
@core: Do I have to…? / Is that necessary? | 问某件事是不是必须做 | Do I have to / Do I need to | 正式：Is it necessary for me to attend?｜随意：Do I really have to?
@core: We don't allow… / You're not allowed to… | 说公司不允许的事 | that / food in the meeting room / personal apps | 正式：Personal devices are not allowed in this area.｜随意：You can't do that here.
