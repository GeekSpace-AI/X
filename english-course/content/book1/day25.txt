@diff: 「做」——make 还是 do？
@d_cn: 中文一个「做」：做决定、做选择、做作业、做调查、做家务……
@d_en: make a decision / make a call / make money / do research / do my homework / do the dishes
@d_why: 中文「做」几乎什么都能搭，英文却分成两个词。大致规律：**make** = 做出、造出一个原来没有的东西或结果（决定、电话、计划、噪音、钱、咖啡）；**do** = 完成一项任务、工作或活动（作业、调查、家务、工作本身）。规律不是 100%，最好的办法是**整块记搭配**：make a decision，不说 do a decision；do homework，不说 make homework。
@d_when: make：a decision / a choice / a call / a plan / a mistake / money / noise / coffee；do：research / homework / the dishes / a good job / your best
@d_vs: **I need to make a decision.** 我得做个决定。\n**I need to do some research.** 我得查些资料。\n✗ do a decision → ✓ make a decision\n✗ make homework → ✓ do homework
@d_task: 选 make 或 do 说：① 我今晚要做作业。② 我得打个电话。③ 别再犯同样的错误。 | ① I have to do my homework tonight. ② I need to make a call. ③ Don't make the same mistake again.
@diff: take——不只是「拿」
@d_cn: 记笔记、休息一下、拍照、洗澡、坐地铁、花时间……
@d_en: take notes / take a break / take a photo / take a shower / take the subway / It takes an hour.
@d_why: 中文用的是不同的动词：「记」笔记、「休息」、「拍」照、「洗」澡、「坐」地铁。英文这些全都可以用 **take**——take 的核心意思是「拿来、占用、进行一次」：拿一段时间来休息，「拿」下一张照片。中国学生常按中文去找动词，说出 write notes、have a rest、wash a shower：前两个能懂但不地道，最后一个完全不对。
@d_when: 开会时负责记录 → take notes；累了 → take a break；拍照 → take a photo；洗澡 → take a shower；交通 → take the bus / the subway（Day 2）
@d_vs: **I'll take notes.** 我来做会议记录。\n**Take a break!** 歇会儿吧！\n**Can you take a photo of us?** 能帮我们拍张照吗？\n✗ I need to wash a shower. → ✓ I need to take a shower.
@d_task: 用 take 说：① 我们休息十分钟吧。② 能帮我们拍张合影吗？ | ① Let's take a ten-minute break. ② Can you take a photo of us?
@diff: get——拿、买、收到、到达
@d_cn: 去拿杯咖啡、去买午饭、收到邮件、几点到公司……
@d_en: I'm going to get some coffee. / Did you get my message? / I get to work at nine.
@d_why: 中文要用「拿 / 买 / 收到 / 到」好几个动词，美国人口语里一个 **get** 全包了，核心意思是「（从没有到）有了 / 到了」。中国学生常按「拿」直译成 take：I'm going to take some coffee.——在英文里这是「我把咖啡带走 / 带过去」。另外，大家爱用课本里的书面词：I received your email. / I arrived at the office at nine.——都没错，但日常说话太正式，用 get 更自然。
@d_when: 去买 / 去拿：get coffee / get lunch；收到：get an email / a message；到达：get to work / get home；做运动、休息：get some exercise / get some rest
@d_vs: **I'm going to get some coffee.** 我去弄杯咖啡。\n**I'm going to take some coffee to Tom.** 我去给张涛送杯咖啡。（take = 带过去）\n**Did you get my message?** 你收到我的消息了吗？\n**What time do you get home?** 你几点到家？
@d_task: 用 get 说：① 我去买午饭。② 你收到我的邮件了吗？③ 我一般八点五十到公司。 | ① I'm going to get lunch. ② Did you get my email? ③ I usually get to the office at 8:50.
@diff: have——不只是「有」
@d_cn: 有个会、吃午饭、喝咖啡、约了牙医、玩得开心……
@d_en: I have a meeting at ten. / Let's have lunch. / I have a dentist appointment. / Have fun!
@d_why: 中文说「我十点开会」「我们吃午饭吧」，动词是「开」「吃」。英文常用 **have**：have a meeting、have lunch、have coffee、have fun。很多人把「开会」直译成 open a meeting——open a meeting 是主持人「宣布会议开始」，不是「参加一个会」。「吃午饭」说 eat lunch 也对，但 have lunch 更常用、更委婉。好消息是：中文「我有个会」里有个「有」，英文正好也用 have。
@d_when: 安排：have a meeting / a call / an appointment；吃喝：have lunch / coffee / a sandwich；经历：have fun / have a good weekend / have a problem
@d_vs: **I have a meeting at ten.** 我十点有个会。\n✗ I open a meeting at ten.\n**Let's have lunch.** 一起吃午饭吧。\n**Have fun this weekend!** 周末玩得开心！
@d_task: 用 have 说：① 我下午三点有个视频会。② 周末玩得开心！ | ① I have a video call at three this afternoon. ② Have fun this weekend!

@core: I have a + 安排 + at + 时间. | 说日程安排 | a meeting / a call / an appointment | 正式：I have a meeting scheduled at 10 a.m.｜随意：Got a meeting at ten.

@core: Take a break! | 劝别人休息一下 | Take a break / Let's take a ten-minute break | 正式：Shall we take a short break?｜随意：Go take a break!
