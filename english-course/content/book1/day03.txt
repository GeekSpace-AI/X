@diff: 「这里有……」——There is / There are，不说 Here has
@d_cn: 告诉别人某个地方有什么东西、有什么设施。
@d_en: There's a printer here. / There are two meeting rooms on this floor.
@d_why: 中文的「有」可以直接放在地点后面：「这里有」「五楼有」。英文不让地点去「有」东西，而是用固定的句型 **There is / There are + 东西 + 地点**：先说「有什么」，地点放到句尾。这里的 There 没有「那里」的意思，只是一个开头。
@d_when: 第一次告诉别人某处有什么：There's a kitchen on this floor. 单数和不可数（coffee、water）用 There is（口语 There's），复数用 There are。
@d_vs: ✗ Here has a printer. → ✓ There's a printer here.\n✗ This floor have two meeting rooms. → ✓ There are two meeting rooms on this floor.\n✗ There have free coffee. → ✓ There's free coffee.
@d_task: 用英文说：「我们公司有一个很大的茶水间，五楼有三台打印机。」 | There's a big kitchen in our office. There are three printers on the fifth floor.
@diff: 「我有」vs「某处有」——I have 还是 There is？
@d_cn: 同样一个「有」：「我有一张门禁卡」是拥有；「抽屉里有一张门禁卡」是某个地方存在某样东西。
@d_en: I have a key card.（我拥有）/ There's a key card in the drawer.（某处存在）
@d_why: 中文一个「有」管两件事，英文把它们分开：**人（或公司）拥有**某样东西 → have / has；**某个地方存在**某样东西 → There is / There are。问「有没有」也一样：问人用 Do you have…?，问地方用 Is there…? 把这两个混在一起，就会说出 There have… 或 Have a restroom here? 这样的句子。
@d_when: 说「我 / 他 / 我们公司有」→ I have / He has / Our company has；说「这里 / 五楼 / 抽屉里有」→ There is / There are。
@d_vs: **Do you have a pen?** 你有笔吗？（问人）\n**Is there a restroom on this floor?** 这层有洗手间吗？（问地方）\n✗ Have a restroom on this floor? ✗ Here have a restroom?
@d_task: 分别用 have 和 There is 说：① 我有两台电脑。② 会议室里有一台电脑。 | ① I have two computers. ② There's a computer in the meeting room.
@diff: 说地点：on the fifth floor／in the meeting room／at the front desk
@d_cn: 中文都是一个「在」：在五楼、在会议室、在前台。
@d_en: on the fifth floor / in the meeting room / at the front desk
@d_why: 中文只要一个「在」，英文要看这个地方是什么「样子」：**on** = 在一个平面、一层上（楼层、左右两边）；**in** = 在一个有边界的空间里面（房间、抽屉、角落）；**at** = 在一个点、一个位置上（前台、工位、走廊尽头）。
@d_when: 楼层、方向：on the fifth floor / on the left；房间、容器：in the kitchen / in the drawer；某个点或办公位置：at the front desk / at my desk / at the end of the hall。
@d_vs: **She's on the fifth floor.** 她在五楼。\n**She's in the meeting room.** 她在会议室（里面）。\n**She's at the front desk.** 她在前台。\n✗ in the fifth floor ✗ on the meeting room
@d_task: 用英文说：「张涛在会议室，Emma 在她的工位上，李凯在六楼。」 | Tom is in the meeting room. Emma is at her desk. Kevin is on the sixth floor.

@core: There's a… / There are… + 地点. | 介绍某个地方有什么 | a kitchen / two meeting rooms / free coffee | 正式：There are two meeting rooms available on this floor.｜随意：There's coffee in the kitchen.
@core: Is there a… on this floor? | 问某处有没有某样设施 | restroom / printer / quiet room | 正式：Excuse me, is there a restroom on this floor?｜随意：Any restrooms up here?
@core: It's next to / near / between… | 说东西在哪里 | next to the kitchen / near the window / between A and B | 正式：It's located next to the kitchen.｜随意：It's right by the kitchen.
@core: It's at the end of the hall, on the left. | 指路：走廊尽头、在左 / 右边 | left / right；hall / row | 正式：It's at the end of the hallway, on your left.｜随意：End of the hall, on the left.
@core: Our team is on the + 序数 + floor. | 说公司、部门在几楼 | fifth / sixth / twelfth | 正式：Our department is located on the fifth floor.｜随意：We're on five.
@core: Let me show you around. | 主动带新人或访客参观 | the office / the warehouse / our floor | 正式：Allow me to show you around.｜随意：Come on, I'll show you around.
