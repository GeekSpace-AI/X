@diff: 「我想……」——I want to（愿望）vs I'm going to（已经决定）
@d_cn: 「我想去……」——可能只是想想，也可能已经决定了、票都买好了。
@d_en: I want to go shopping.（想，但还没定）/ I'm going to visit my parents.（已经决定了）
@d_why: 中文的「我想」「我要」「我打算」边界很模糊：「我周末想回家」可能只是想想，也可能车票都买好了。英文分得很清楚：**want to** 只表达愿望，没说到底做不做；**be going to** 表示已经决定、有了安排。所以 Emma 说 I want to go shopping, but I'm not sure.（想去，还没定），林晓说 I'm going to visit my parents.（票都买了）。如果计划早定了你却说 I want to…，对方会以为你还在犹豫。
@d_when: 只是想、还没决定 → I want to… / I'd like to…；已经决定、做了准备 → I'm going to…
@d_vs: **I want to visit my parents.** 我想去看我爸妈。（愿望）\n**I'm going to visit my parents.** 我要去看我爸妈。（已经定了）\n**I want to go shopping, but I'm not sure.** 我想去逛街，但还没定。\n✗ I want go shopping.
@d_task: 用英文说：①「我想学开车，不过还没想好。」②「我下个月要去北京，票已经买了。」 | ① I want to learn to drive, but I'm not sure. ② I'm going to visit Beijing next month. I already have the ticket.
@diff: 「我发给你」——当场答应用 will，早有计划用 be going to
@d_cn: 「我（会）给你发」——是说话这一刻才决定、答应你的，还是早就计划好的？
@d_en: OK, I'll send you the schedule.（当场答应）/ I'm going to visit my parents.（早就计划好）
@d_why: 中文的「会」「要」「就」不太区分「什么时候决定的」。英文要分：**will**（常缩成 'll）用于**说话这一刻才做的决定**，或者**答应、承诺**对方——林晓问能不能看日程，Tom 当场说 OK, I'll send you the schedule.；Emma 听了林晓的建议，当场说 I'll do that.。**be going to** 用于**说话之前就已经决定的计划**。没把握的打算，常说 I'll probably… / Maybe I'll…
@d_when: 别人提出请求，你当场答应 → I'll…；临时想到要做 → I'll…；说早就定好的打算 → I'm going to…
@d_vs: **Can I see the schedule? — OK, I'll send it to you.** 当场答应\n**I'm going to send the schedule on Monday.** 早就打算好的\n**Maybe I'll stay home.** 也许就待在家吧。（临时的想法）\nCan I see the schedule? — OK, I'm going to send it.（不自然：像是早就打算好了）
@d_task: 同事说「打印机又没纸了」，你当场说：「我去拿点纸。」 | I'll get some paper.
@diff: 「我周三要见客户」——I'm meeting a client on Wednesday.
@d_cn: 说已经约好、定了时间的安排：「我周三要见客户」「周四我们开会讨论」。
@d_en: I'm meeting a client on Wednesday. / We're discussing it on Thursday.
@d_why: 中文用「要 + 时间」就够了，很多人直译成 I will meet a client on Wednesday.——不算错，但美国人说**和别人约好、日历上写着的安排**，最常用 **am / is / are + -ing**，再加上具体时间。句子里的 on Wednesday 告诉对方你说的是将来，不是「现在正在见」。问别人的安排也常这样问：What are you doing this weekend?
@d_when: 已经约好时间、地点、人的安排：见客户、开会、出差、看医生、和朋友吃饭。
@d_vs: **I'm meeting a client on Wednesday.** 我周三要见客户。（已经约好）\n**What are you doing this weekend?** 你这周末干什么？\n**I'm meeting a client now.** 我正在见客户。（没有将来的时间，就是「现在正在」）\nI meet a client on Wednesday.（容易听成每周三的习惯）
@d_task: 用英文说：「我周五下午要和 Emma 吃午饭。」再问同事：「你今晚干什么？」 | I'm having lunch with Emma on Friday afternoon. / What are you doing tonight?

@core: What are you doing this weekend? | 问别人周末或某个时间的计划 | this weekend / tonight / after work | 正式：Do you have any plans for the weekend?｜随意：Any plans this weekend?
@core: I'm going to + 动作. | 说已经决定的计划 | visit my parents / clean my room / see a movie | 正式：I'm planning to visit my parents.｜随意：I'm gonna visit my parents.（只在口语里说）
@core: I'm + -ing + 时间. | 说已经约好的安排 | meeting a client on Wednesday / having lunch with Emma on Friday | 正式：I have a meeting with a client on Wednesday.｜随意：Meeting a client Wednesday.
@core: I want to…, but I'm not sure. | 有想法，但还没决定 | go shopping / take a trip / buy a new phone | 正式：I'd like to…, but I haven't decided yet.｜随意：Thinking about going shopping. Not sure yet.
@core: OK, I'll + 动作. | 当场答应、承诺 | send you the schedule / call you back / get some paper | 正式：Certainly. I'll send you the schedule this afternoon.｜随意：Sure, will do!
@core: Maybe I'll… / I'll probably… | 说没把握的打算 | stay home / take an early train / go shopping | 正式：I'll most likely stay home.｜随意：Probably just stay home.
@core: Good idea! | 赞同别人的建议 | Good idea! / Great idea! / That's a good idea. | 正式：That sounds like a good plan.｜随意：Love it!
